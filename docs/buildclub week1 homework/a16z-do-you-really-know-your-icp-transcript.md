## Episode summary

Your ideal customer profile (ICP) is the north star for your entire company: it determines who
you're building for and selling to. Though most growth-stage founders think they know who their
ICP is, very few know how to update and refine it to keep the company focused as they grow.
The panel breaks down why ICP misalignment is often the hidden cause of common problems across
the company — from pipeline gaps and bloated marketing spend to stalled product roadmaps — and
how to fix it, including why ICPs matter even more in the AI era.
